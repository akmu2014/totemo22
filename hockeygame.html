🏒 Ice Hockey Showdown

How to play

You're the player with the yellow ring. Skate into the puck and it stays on your stick. SHOOT (or F): tap for a quick wrist shot, hold for a harder one. It goes exactly where the yellow target is — it starts on the side the goalie leaves open, and while you hold SHOOT the joystick moves it (push toward the net for top shelf). DECOY (X) fakes a shot to fool the goalie and defenders. SHOVE (V) knocks an opponent off the puck. Watch out: opponents can poke it away! When the puck is close, tap STEAL (or R) to lunge with your stick and take it.
📱 Phone: joystick on the left to skate, swipe on the right to turn the camera, pinch to zoom.
💻 Computer: WASD or arrows to skate, drag the mouse to turn the camera, wheel to zoom.
Tap PASS (or Space) for a lead pass to your teammate — he gets open on his own. When he has the puck, the button says CALL: tap it and he passes it back. At every faceoff, tap WIN FACEOFF right when the ref drops the puck — too early and you lose the draw! Tap 🎥 for other camera views, 🎵 to turn the music on or off.

Hockey rules
🕒 3 periods of 2 minutes. Most goals wins. Tied? Overtime — next goal wins.
🔵 Offside: don't skate over the other team's blue line before the puck.
🧊 Icing: shooting from your half across the far goal line (not into the net) means a faceoff in your end.
🏒 Play restarts with a faceoff after goals and whistles.
