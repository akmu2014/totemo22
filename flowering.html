Flowering

4th FULL ALBUM

04-07-2026

AKMU’s fourth full-length album is about 'Flowering'. For the beauty of growing, for the joy of finding your own season, for the happiness that comes after a long journey, and for the moments when life finally begins to bloom. Filled with heartfelt melodies and stories of growth, this album celebrates the beauty of discovering happiness in the present and blooming together along the way.
